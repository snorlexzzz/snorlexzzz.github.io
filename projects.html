Central Bank Sentiment Analysis, Feb 2025

This project studies how central bank communications influence interest rate decisions. It focuses on speeches from U.S. Federal Reserve Chairs (Powell, Yellen, Bernanke) and European Central Bank officials. Using FinBERT, a financial sentiment analysis model, speeches are classified as Hawkish (Bullish), Neutral, or Dovish (Bearish) (multi-class classification). The goal is to test whether the sentiment expressed in these speeches aligns with the subsequent interest rate changes, providing insight into how monetary policy signals are communicated and interpreted.
